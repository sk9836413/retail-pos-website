# Licences

TGS POS and its add-on apps are sold per user through Microsoft AppSource. Microsoft bills you monthly or yearly with your other Microsoft subscriptions.

## Who needs a licence

Every Business Central user who works with the app needs a licence for it:

* each **POS terminal** that logs in to Business Central with its own user (most stores use one Business Central user per till or tablet; the staff log on to the POS with their staff ID);
* back-office users who use the POS pages (setup, offers, statements, reports).

Business Central users who never open the POS pages (for example the accountant) don't need one.
Each add-on app (Hospitality, India GST, Payment Terminals, E-Commerce) has its own licence for the users who use it.

## Buy and assign

1. Buy the plan on AppSource (a one-month free trial is available). The licences appear in the **Microsoft 365 admin center** under **Billing → Licenses**.
2. In the Microsoft 365 admin center, assign the licences to users (**Users → Active users → Licenses and apps**).
3. In Business Central, give the same users the permission set (**RPOS ALL** and the add-on permission sets). The licence is the upper limit;
   the permission set decides what each user may do.

Users without a licence can't open the app's pages. Your partner's delegated admins and service-to-service integrations
(the QR ordering / Swiggy-Zomato connector) work without a user licence.
