# Offers, coupons, loyalty and gift cards

## Offers

Open **Offers** and create an offer of one of these types:

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Discount Offer | 10% off all shoes |
| Multibuy | 3 for 99, or 10% off from 5 pieces |
| Mix & Match | any shirt + any trousers for 1,999; buy 2, the cheapest free |
| Total Discount | 5% off bills over 2,000 |
| Points Offer | double loyalty points on a brand |
| Tender Discount | 5% off when paying with a certain bank's card |

Every offer has a priority, a date and time window, weekdays, stores or a **store group**, member club / tier, and can require a coupon.
**Offer Performance** shows how much each offer sold and gave away. Items can be excluded with *No Discount Allowed* on the item card.

## Coupons

Coupons give a % or amount off, or unlock an offer. Options: minimum purchase, maximum uses, members only, validity.
A coupon with *Issue on Purchase Over* is a **template**: the POS prints a personal coupon for the next visit on qualifying receipts.
**Issue to All Members** sends personal coupons to every member.

## Loyalty

* **Member Clubs**: value of one point, minimum points to redeem, points expiry, and **tier basis**: lifetime points or the points earned
  in the last 12 months. The scheduled job moves members **up and down** the tiers.
* **Member Schemes (tiers)**: points earned per currency unit, member discount %, upgrade at lifetime points.
* **Members**: one account per customer or family. **Member Cards** lets an account have several cards (block a lost card and give a new one).
  **Family / Contacts** lets family members use the account and, if allowed, redeem points. Members are found by card number or mobile number.
* **Points as payment**: choose the points tender on the POS. Switch on *Ask OTP before redeeming points* in the
  [SMS / WhatsApp Setup](messaging) to send the member an OTP first.
* **POS & Member Messages**: messages shown at log-on (to staff) or when a member is identified (for example a birthday greeting).

## Gift cards and credit vouchers

* Sell a gift card with **Gift Card Sale** (new card or top-up). It is a liability until it is used.
* Pay with the gift card tender. **Gift Card Balance** checks the balance.
* Refunds can go to a **credit voucher**. Validity periods are in **TGS POS Setup**.
