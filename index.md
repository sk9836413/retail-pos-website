# TGS POS for Business Central

A complete point of sale for shops, chains and restaurants, built into **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central online**.
Your sales, stock, customers and accounts live in one system. There is no separate POS server and no store database to look after.

[हिन्दी में पढ़ें](hi/)

## Apps

| App | For |
|---|---|
| **TGS POS** | Every store: fast web POS, offers and coupons, loyalty, gift cards, returns, cash management, statements, stock, replenishment, reports |
| **TGS POS - Hospitality** | Restaurants, cafés and quick service: tables, kitchen display, courses, meal deals, QR table ordering, self-order kiosk |
| **TGS POS - India GST** | Indian businesses: GST on every sale, tax invoices, e-invoice (IRN), e-way bill, GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B |
| **TGS POS - Payment Terminals** | Card terminals (Pine Labs, Razorpay POS, Adyen, Stripe, Square, SumUp) and dynamic UPI QR (Razorpay, PhonePe) |
| **TGS POS - E-Commerce** | Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, and Swiggy / Zomato through UrbanPiper |
| **TGS POS - Tally Migration** | Moving from Tally: ledgers, customers, vendors, items, opening balances, open bills and stock in a few steps ([how](help/tally-migration)) |

The Hardware Bridge (a free Windows helper for receipt printers, cash drawers, scales and customer displays) is a separate download.
